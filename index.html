How to use English to Assamese Translation

English to Assamese Translation

This free tool turns English text into Assamese script in about a second. Paste a word, a sentence or a short paragraph, and the Assamese translation appears next to it. It also reads Assamese, so you can use the same page for Assamese to English translation.

How to translate English to Assamese

  1. Pick English as the first language and Assamese as the second (these are set by default).
  2. Type or paste your text, or tap Speak and say it aloud.
  3. Read the Assamese translation, then use Copy to paste it into a chat, form or document.

Assamese to English Translation

To translate Assamese to English, press the swap button between the language menus. Your Assamese text goes into the input box and the English meaning shows on the right. This is handy for reading Assamese messages, notices and news. Looking for a quick Assamese to English meaning of one word? Type just that word.

Translate any language into Assamese

Hindi, Bengali, Bodo, Nepali and many more languages can be translated into Assamese. If you do not know the source language, choose Detect language. Searching for English to Assamese on your phone? This page is built to work smoothly on small screens.

About the Assamese language

Assamese (অসমীয়া) is the main language of Assam and one of the 22 scheduled languages of India. It is spoken by more than 15 million people and is written in the Assamese-Bengali script. Machine translation handles everyday text well, but names, idioms and formal documents may need a human check.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I translate English to Assamese online?

Type or paste your English text in the left box. The Assamese translation appears in the right box within a second. Use the Translate button if you want to refresh it.

Is this English to Assamese translation tool free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no sign-up and no app to install.

Can I use it for Assamese to English translation?

Yes. Press the swap button between the two language menus. The tool then works as an Assamese to English translator, and your translated text moves into the input box.

Which other languages can I translate into Assamese?

You can translate Hindi, Bengali, Bodo, Nepali, Urdu and many other languages into Assamese. Choose Detect language and the tool finds the source language for you.

Is the translation 100% accurate?

It is machine translation, so it works well for everyday words and sentences. For legal, medical or official documents, ask a human translator to check the result.

Can I speak instead of typing?

Yes. Tap the Speak button and allow microphone access in your browser. Voice input works best in Chrome on Android and desktop.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. The page and the translator are built for small screens as well as desktop.
