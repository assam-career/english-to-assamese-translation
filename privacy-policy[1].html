Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how English to Assamese Translation handles information when you use this website.

Text you translate

The text you type or speak is sent to third-party translation services only to produce your translation. We do not create user accounts and we do not store your translated text on our own servers.

Log files and analytics

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit. This data is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and you can block cookies in your browser settings.

Microphone

If you use the Speak button, your browser asks for microphone permission. Speech is processed by your browser or device speech service.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

Contact

Email us at your-email@gmail.com or use the Contact Us page.

About

English to Assamese Translation is a free online tool to translate English to Assamese and Assamese to English. You can also translate any language into Assamese.
