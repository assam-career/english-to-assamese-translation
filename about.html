About Us

English to Assamese Translation is a free online tool that helps you translate English to Assamese and Assamese to English. You can also translate other languages, such as Hindi, Bengali and Bodo, into Assamese.

Why we made it

Many people look for a simple, fast Assamese translator that works on a phone. We built this page for students, job seekers, families and anyone who needs to read or write Assamese without installing an app.

How it works

The text you enter is sent to online machine translation services and the result is shown on the page. Machine translation is useful for everyday text, but please check important documents with a human translator.
